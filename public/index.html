ABOUT v0.1

A starting point for better journeys.

An independent local transport guide. Map points are approximate orientation landmarks, not verified stops, wharves or routes.

What’s connected?

OpenStreetMap provides the basemap. Official travel information opens on its publisher’s website. Live road conditions, bus positions, ferry departures and cycle networks are not imported yet.

Sources checked 22 September 2026. ORC timetable data is licensed CC BY 4.0. Live-feed access and dataset-specific QLDC terms still need confirmation.

On your phone & offline

Use your browser’s install option, or on iPhone use Share → Add to Home Screen. After an online visit, the app and private drafts work offline; map tiles and official websites need a connection.

Your privacy

No accounts or analytics. Drafts stay in this browser until you delete them or clear site data. Location is requested only when you tap My location and is not saved. Map tiles are requested from OpenStreetMap, which receives your IP address and the area viewed.
